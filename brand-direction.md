# Nashy Trellis production direction

The live build is now being developed from the approved desktop and mobile mockups.

## Production priorities

- Use genuine Nashy Trellis installation photography as proof of workmanship.
- Keep the visual system architectural: graphite, warm stone, aluminium silver and restrained Nashy bronze.
- Keep WhatsApp and Free Quote as the primary conversion actions.
- Mobile experience must be intentionally designed, not merely a stacked desktop layout.
- Product architecture: Trellis Gates, Window Security, Burglar Bars, Monkey-Proof Gates, Balustrades.
- Trust architecture: 10-year guarantee, free quotation, no-deposit/payment positioning, full-house discounts and written-quote promise, subject to final terms.
- Build around Durban/KZN local intent and coastal aluminium positioning.
